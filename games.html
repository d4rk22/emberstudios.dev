Meowers

Mice vs. Cats Tower Defense

Defend your territory against waves of adorable but relentless cats! Place mouse towers along the path to shoot cheese and stop the feline invasion. Earn gold from defeated cats to build and upgrade your defenses. Each tower has unique abilities — from rapid-fire cheese launchers to electric zappers and yarn slingers.

  • 5 Tower Types: Basic, Rapid, Heavy, Electric, and Yarn — each with unique strengths
  • Strategic Depth: Place towers wisely along the winding path to maximize your defense
  • Charming Art: Cute hand-drawn cats, mice, and a colorful world full of flowers
  • Built for iOS: Native iPhone and iPad experience with smooth touch controls
