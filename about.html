About Ember Studios

Our Story

Ember Studios is a one-person indie studio born from a lifelong love of technology. Since picking up an original iPhone in 2007, the dream was always to build for it — and now that dream is a reality.

Growing up geeking out on gadgets sparked a passion that never faded. After years of imagining what could be, Ember Studios was founded to finally bring those ideas to life. Everything from Ember Studios is crafted with care, built by a developer who's thrilled to finally share these creations with the world.

We hope you enjoy what we create.

What We Do

Ember Studios focuses exclusively on the Apple ecosystem, building native games and apps for iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. Our focus areas include:

  • iOS Games: Fun, engaging experiences that respect your time and attention
  • Apple TV Apps: Beautifully native experiences for the living room, like Airdate
  • Quality First: We believe in polish over pace — every interaction is considered, every detail matters
  • Charming Design: Colorful, hand-crafted worlds that bring a smile to your face

Our Values

  • Craftsmanship: We take pride in building software that feels right
  • Privacy: We respect our users' data and only collect what's necessary
  • Indie Spirit: Small studio, big ambitions — built with love, not by committee
