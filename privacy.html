Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Ember Studios, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our mobile applications and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our games. Any information you voluntarily provide is limited to:

  • Contact information if you reach out to us for support
  • Feedback or bug reports you submit

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our apps, we may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Game progress, session duration, features used, and in-app interactions
  • Performance Data: Crash reports and technical diagnostics to improve app stability

1.3 Advertising Data

Our apps display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS)
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • Device and network information
  • Ad interaction data

For more information about AdMob's data practices, please review Google's Privacy Policy. Advertising applies to our games only; Reelhouse contains no advertising.

1.4 Reelhouse (Apple TV), formerly Airdate

Reelhouse is a player for your own Plex library. It has no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and you don’t create an account with us. The full, detailed policy is at reelhouse.net/privacy. In short:

  • Plex: Reelhouse signs in through Plex’s own link page and talks to plex.tv and your Plex Media Server to browse and play your library. The access token stays in your device’s Keychain; we never receive it. See the Plex Privacy Policy.
  • Air dates, cast and movie details come from TheTVDB through a small service we run on Cloudflare. The app sends show and movie IDs, and for shows without an ID, their titles, plus cast names and your device’s country and language. No account, token or device ID is sent. Cached answers aren’t linked to you and expire about 30 days after last use.
  • Send Feedback (only when you press Send) sends your message, your Plex username and profile name, your server’s name, device model, app version and settings, and recent playback logs that include the titles you played recently. Reports are kept in our private Cloudflare storage for 30 days; if you write a message, a summary goes into our private issue tracker.
  • Volume Levelling sharing is off by default. If you turn on Share Loudness Measurements, only a fingerprint of the media file and loudness numbers are shared, never titles or account details.
  • Preferences such as skip settings can sync between your devices through Apple’s iCloud, which we can’t see.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our games
  • Respond to your support requests and communications
  • Analyze app usage and performance to enhance user experience
  • Fix bugs and resolve technical issues
  • Display relevant advertisements (through AdMob)
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party services like Google AdMob for advertising, analytics providers for app performance monitoring
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights and users' safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. Your Choices and Rights

4.1 Advertising Preferences

You can control personalized advertising through your device settings:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Limit Ad Tracking
  • iOS (Alternate): Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → Personalized Ads

You can also opt out of personalized ads through the Digital Advertising Alliance at www.aboutads.info/choices.

4.2 Premium Purchase

You can purchase a one-time in-app purchase to permanently remove all advertisements from our games.

4.3 Data Deletion

To request deletion of any data we may have collected about you, please contact us at support@emberstudios.dev. We will process your request within 30 days.

5. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will promptly delete such information.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. These countries may have different data protection laws. By using our apps, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States and other countries where we operate.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of our apps after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

9. Third-Party Services

Our apps use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (games): For displaying advertisements — Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: For in-app purchases and app distribution — Privacy Policy
  • Plex (Reelhouse): For account sign-in and access to your own media server — Privacy Policy
  • TheTVDB (Reelhouse): For air dates, cast and movie details — Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare (Reelhouse): Hosts our metadata, feedback and loudness service — Privacy Policy

These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.
